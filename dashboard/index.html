READING THIS DASHBOARD

Data notes & definitions

What the numbers mean

Item sales: sum of prices for delivered order items, excluding freight. This is not net revenue or profit. No currency conversion is performed.

Delivered orders: distinct orders with an item in the selected category and purchase period. Sales per order: item sales divided by that order count.

Time: purchase month using the original source timestamps. Missing activity months are not shown as zero sales.

Categories and missing records

Missing product categories are labeled “Unknown.” The source includes 610 products without categories and 775 orders without items. Those no-item orders are retained in the cleaned data but do not appear in this item-based dashboard.

Category order counts overlap because one order can contain several categories. For all-category totals, this dashboard uses distinct-order monthly totals.

Source and verification

This is a local snapshot of the verified Gold outputs. The separate incremental simulation, including its artificial price changes, is excluded. Changing a filter does not contact AWS or run Spark.
